Frequently Asked Questions

Is it normal for gums to bleed?

No, bleeding is never normal. It is the first sign of inflammation and an indication that bacteria are attacking the tissues supporting your teeth. If your gums bleed when you brush, you need a professional assessment.

Does this affect dental implants?

Yes, in fact, it is one of the leading causes of implant loss (peri-implantitis). That is why we never place implants without first ensuring that your periodontal system is completely healthy.

Is it connected to overall health?

Absolutely. Periodontitis is scientifically linked to a higher risk of cardiovascular issues, diabetes complications, and even risks during pregnancy.
