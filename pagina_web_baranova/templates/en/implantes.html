Frequently Asked Questions

Do I really need an implant, or are there other alternatives?

Every smile is unique. If we can save your natural tooth safely and durably, that will always be our first choice. We will only recommend an implant if it is the most predictable and beneficial option for your long-term health.

Is the process of getting an implant painful?

This is the most common concern, and the answer will reassure you: no, it does not hurt. We work with local anesthesia and minimally invasive techniques. We will ensure you are comfortable at all times and plan your recovery so you barely notice any discomfort.

What if my gums are not in good condition?

That is a great question! Your gums are the foundation of your mouth. If there is any issue (such as inflammation or periodontitis), we will treat that first to heal the foundation. Placing an implant in healthy gums is the secret to making it last a lifetime.
