Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get orthodontic treatment if I've already had it before?

Absolutely. In fact, many of our patients come to us for retreatment. We will thoroughly evaluate why your previous treatment failed or relapsed (usually because the bite or function wasn't properly adjusted) to ensure that this time, the results are permanent.

Clear aligners or traditional braces?

The technique (aligners or brackets) is simply a tool. What truly matters is the treatment plan designed by the specialist. We work with both systems, carefully selecting the one that best suits your specific clinical goals.
